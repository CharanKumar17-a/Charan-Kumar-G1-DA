<h3 id="project1">Project 1: Vision Zero Chicago — Safety Capital Investment Prioritization</h3>
<p>
<p>
<strong>Vision Zero Chicago</strong> is a data-driven decision-support system designed to help prioritize road-safety investments across Chicago's high-crash corridors. The project uses historical crash data, risk forecasting, FHWA safety treatments, and optimization techniques to recommend an efficient and equitable allocation of limited capital budgets while supporting transportation engineers and city decision-makers.
</p>
